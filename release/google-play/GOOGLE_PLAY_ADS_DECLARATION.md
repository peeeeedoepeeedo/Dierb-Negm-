# Google Play Ads Declaration — Dierb Online

Dierb Online contains first-party sponsored/featured marketplace placements such as banners, featured stores/products/listings and sponsored content when enabled by platform settings.

There is currently no third-party mobile advertising SDK in the Android shell.

For the Play Console "Contains ads" question, select **Yes** while sponsored placements are shown to users. Do not describe them as an external ad network if none is used.

If a third-party ad SDK is added later, update the declaration, Data Safety answers, privacy policy and SDK disclosures before that release.
