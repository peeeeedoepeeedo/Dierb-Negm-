# Google Play Content Rating Notes — Dierb Online

The live Play questionnaire wording can change; answer the current questionnaire based on the submitted build. These notes identify product features that matter to the rating.

## Present in the app
- User-generated community posts and comments.
- Questions and answers.
- User reviews.
- Classified, car, property and job listings.
- User-to-user/business messaging.
- Reporting and admin moderation tools.
- Sponsored/featured marketplace content.

## Product policy
The platform rules prohibit illegal, fraudulent, threatening, hateful, sexually exploitative, abusive and privacy-violating content. Users can report content and moderation can remove content or restrict accounts.

## Questionnaire guidance
- Declare user-generated content where asked.
- Declare communication/messaging features where asked.
- Do not state that all UGC is pre-moderated if it can be published before review.
- Do state that reporting/moderation controls exist where the questionnaire asks.
- Do not declare gambling, real-money gaming, alcohol/tobacco sales, sexual content or other regulated categories unless a future production feature actually permits them.
- If marketplace listings can expose content not intended for children, do not position the app as children-directed without a separate children-safe product and policy review.

Re-check the final production categories and moderation configuration before submitting the questionnaire.
